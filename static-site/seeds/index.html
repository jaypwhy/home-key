Phase 0 — Planning

The Seed Program

Twelve small starter cohorts — seeds — of 5–25 participants each, planted in neighborhoods across the City of San Diego. Each seed grows the same whole system: free housing, health, food, hygiene, regenerative income, and travel, with wellness animals included from day one.

Honest status: no participants housed yet, no funds raised yet. We are building the partnerships and legal structure first — that is what Phase 0 means.

The program in one paragraph

Everyone has a place to call home. Project Home Key's Seed Program will start 12 small starter cohorts ("seeds") of 5–25 participants each in neighborhoods across the City of San Diego, delivering free housing + health + food + hygiene + regenerative income + zero-entropy travel as one navigable system — with wellness-animal accommodations built in, not added on. Budgets carry quadrupled generational padding so participants arrive stabilized and build their own cushion, and the design is evidence-backed (Housing First retention commonly cited at 73–88%; Finland's national Housing First results).

Entity status — clearly pending: we propose to launch under fiscal sponsorship and graduate to an independent 501(c)(3), pending final owner confirmation and legal counsel validation. Stated exactly: fiscal sponsorship -> independent 501(c)(3) PROPOSED, pending final confirmation and legal counsel validation.

We are in Phase 0 now: no one housed yet, no funds promised — seeking to align with partners before anything launches.

The six pillars, as one system

A seed participant receives the whole system at once — never a list of referrals. The six pillars, in the program's own words:

  • Free Independent Personal Housing

    A private home of your own in the cohort neighborhood — no rent, no eviction clock.

  • Free Universal Health

    Referral into existing community care — FQHC and sliding-scale clinics, not our own clinic.

  • Free Food Resources

    Coordination with existing distributions near each cohort — not a parallel food system.

  • Free Hygiene Resources

    Showers, laundry, and personal care mapped and reachable from every seed pocket.

  • Free Regenerative Independent Personal Income

    A flexible living stipend plus the Future Fund starter grant path, so income exceeds outgo and every participant builds a cushion.

  • Free Zero Entropy Travel

    Reduced-fare enrollment support (PRONTO LIFE and sibling programs) plus remote-first work, so getting around is never a barrier.

Wellness-animal accommodations are built in, not added on: pet-friendly housing is a program rule, every cohort budgets pet setup and monthly pet care, and settling-in padding covers people and animals. See the Pilot Proposal for the full build spec.

Generational padding, not tight budgets

  • Budgets are quadrupled — padding sized to end housing instability permanently, for this generation and the next.
  • Cleaning services and connectivity are permanent rights — standing budget lines, never removed or reduced.
  • Wellness animals are requirements, not extras — pet setup and monthly pet care are in every cohort budget.
  • Remote-first positions and flexible living stipends — the stipend flexes to each household's circumstance, and income is designed to exceed outgo so every participant builds their own rolling cushion.

All budget figures live on the Pilot Proposal page — framework estimates, not commitments. No new dollar figures are introduced here.

Phased roadmap

Each phase is gated: we report honestly whether or not targets are met before anything moves forward.

  1. Phase 0 — now · 0 participants

    Partnerships + legal structure

    Align with partner organizations across the five types below, finalize the entity (fiscal sponsorship → independent 501(c)(3), pending), and complete real-cost verification. No one is housed and no funds are promised during this phase.

  2. Phase 1 — next · the first wave of 12 seed cohorts, 5–25 participants each

    San Diego city seeds

    The first wave of small starter cohorts launches in neighborhoods across the City of San Diego, with later waves guided by what each wave shows; every cohort receives the full six-pillar system with wellness animals built in. Retention and outcomes are tracked and reported honestly.

  3. Later phases — framework, not commitment

    Scale only what the seeds prove

    Growth beyond the 12 seeds — more cohorts, wider geography, deeper funding — is gated on what Phase 1 actually shows. Everything described for later phases is a planning framework, not a commitment: targets, timelines, and scale will be reported honestly whether or not they are met.

What we ask of each partner type

Five partner types, five one-page briefs — condensed here to what we ask, what partners gain, and what each relationship honestly is not.

We are mapping candidate partner organizations across these five types; no partnerships are announced. Organizations under identification are candidates only — never presented as committed partners or endorsers.

  • Brief A — Shelter / Housing

    What we ask

    (1) A referral pathway — a named contact and agreed process for bidirectional referrals (including via Coordinated Entry where applicable). (2) Guidance on pet-inclusive units: which beds/units can take participants with animals, and what would make more of them possible.

    What the partner gains

    Navigator co-listing with source credit on our verified Resource Navigator; participants who arrive with padded support (cleaning, connectivity, stipends, pet care) rather than arriving with nothing; invitation to participate in honest evidence tracking (retention reported whether or not targets are met).

    What this is NOT

    Not a replacement for your funding and not a funded program yet — Phase 0, seeking to align. We cannot promise placements, timelines, or dollars.

  • Brief B — Food

    What we ask

    Distribution coordination with Seed pockets: share regular distribution points/schedules near cohort neighborhoods; agree how participants find sites and what to do when schedules shift.

    What the partner gains

    Navigator co-listing with source credit (every entry carries source + check date); participants oriented to your rules (hours, what to bring) so fewer wasted trips; evidence participation in a documented pilot model.

    What this is NOT

    Not a replacement for your funding and not a food buyer yet — Phase 0, seeking to align. No volume or funding commitments.

  • Brief C — Health (FQHC / sliding-scale)

    What we ask

    FQHC / sliding-scale referral: a named referral contact, how uninsured participants enroll (including presumptive/temporary enrollment if you offer it), and any care-coordination touchpoint for participants with complex needs.

    What the partner gains

    Navigator co-listing with source credit; participants arriving with connectivity, transport fare support, and stipends that reduce no-show drivers; honest outcomes tracking shared back.

    What this is NOT

    Not a replacement for your funding and not a payer contract — Phase 0, seeking to align. No patient-volume or payment promises.

  • Brief D — Transit

    What we ask

    Reduced-fare program coordination: how Seed participants enroll (PRONTO LIFE, senior/disability reduced fare, youth pass where eligible), group/institutional pass options if any, and a contact for troubleshooting enrollment.

    What the partner gains

    Navigator co-listing with source credit; riders supported to enroll correctly (fewer failed applications); participation in a documented model linking fare access to housing stability.

    What this is NOT

    Not a replacement for public transit funding and not a bulk fare purchase yet — Phase 0, seeking to align. No ridership or revenue promises.

  • Brief E — Animal Welfare

    What we ask

    (1) Pet food pantry partnership: coordinate pantry access near Seed pockets. (2) Safety-net foster coordination: an agreed referral path for temporary foster during housing transitions, with reunion as the goal.

    What the partner gains

    Navigator co-listing with source credit; pet-owning participants arriving with pet budgets (food, vet, supplies) rather than empty-handed; joint evidence on keeping people and animals together through housing transitions.

    What this is NOT

    Not a replacement for your funding and not a funded program yet — Phase 0, seeking to align. No participation-volume or funding promises.

What we can and cannot promise

We CANNOT promise

Funding amounts or timelines (all figures are framework estimates); any named philanthropic funder (owner privacy directive — never name or research funders); placements, referrals accepted, or outcomes; anything presented as secured.

We CAN promise

Honest partnership (plain language, real limits stated up front); a verified Navigator listing with source credit and check dates; the phased seed plan (Phase 0 now: 0 participants; each phase gated, reported whether or not targets are met); wellness animals treated as family throughout; cleaning and connectivity held as permanent rights in our own budgets.
