  • HUD - Assistance Animal Rights under the Fair Housing Act

    Right in effect - federal enforcement narrowed May 2026

    Honesty flag: HUD's FHEO enforcement guidance (May 22, 2026) pursues animal-accommodation complaints only for animals individually trained to perform disability-related tasks - untrained ESAs are no longer prioritized by HUD enforcement, though Fair Housing Act / Section 504 obligations and private legal action still exist

    Under the Fair Housing Act, a housing provider must let you keep an assistance animal - a trained service animal or an emotional support animal - as a reasonable accommodation, even in no-pets housing, with no pet deposits or breed/size limits, with only narrow legal exceptions.

    Who it is for
    You or a household member must have a disability, and the animal must work for, assist, or provide emotional support that alleviates the disability; a letter from a doctor, therapist, or social worker is the standard supporting document
    How to apply
    Ask your housing provider in writing for a reasonable accommodation; if denied, file a complaint with HUD's Fair Housing and Equal Opportunity office at (800) 669-9777
    Phone
    (800) 669-9777
    Website
    https://www.hud.gov/helping-americans/assistance-animals

    Source: https://www.hud.gov/helping-americans/assistance-animals · Checked 2026-09-05
