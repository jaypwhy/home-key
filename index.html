What Pierce Collabs is

Pierce Collabs is a collaboration network — a growing set of projects ("nodes") connected by named, written agreements ("plugs"), each one plain-language, legally grounded, and severable by design. The first project is Project Home Key: a "Soft Landing" initiative and a new-norm movement — free housing, free health, free food & hygiene resources, free regenerative personal income, and free zero-entropy travel, delivered as one navigable system (RUHBI), starting in San Diego, California, and built as a model meant to be replicated everywhere.

How the network works — nodes and plugs

Nodes are the containers and projects: a website, a development, a program, a small business engine. Each node stands on its own — no home depends on a business decision.

Plugs are the connections between nodes: every one is a named, written valve with a legal basis and a plain-language explanation — never an unmarked pipe, and every bridge severable.

Design law: flexibility over rigidity — rules are defaults with add-only mix-ins, never walls. Cleaning and connectivity are rights. Wellness animals are requirements. Every figure is quadrupled for generational padding against verified real costs. Philanthropic privacy by default.
